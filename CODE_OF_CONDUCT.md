# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming community, AgentCrash
pledges to make participation in the project, in both contributions and
discussion, a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for the
community include:

- Participating in discussions in good faith, with intention to improve the
  project.
- Demonstrating empathy when interacting with other contributors, whether
  clearly visible or not.
- Giving constructive feedback on others' contributions, and accepting
  constructive feedback with grace.
- Being careful in the words we choose and avoiding dismissive language.
- Forgiving mistakes, learning from them, and moving on.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language, including when giving and receiving
  feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting, spamming, or making ad-hominem attacks.
- Harassing or making derogatory personal comments (on appearance, age,
  race/ethnicity, gender expression, nationality, religion, sexual orientation,
  ability, or any other dimension of personal identity).
- Posting or threatening other contributors' personal information ("doxxing").
- Stalking, violating confidentiality, or using forged evidence to discredit
  another contributor.
- Publishing a security report intended to be private (see SECURITY.md).
- Dismissing another contributors' report because of who they are rather than
  the evidence.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official spaces of AgentCrash, including
issue trackers, pull requests, maintainer discussions, and any official social
media or community channels run by the project.

## Enforcement

Initial reports are handled by the project owner. Reports can be sent to the
contact named on the issue form or to security@example.invalid for
security-related conduct. Maintainers will investigate and may escalate to the
owner for a decision. The project reserves the right to remove posts, block
contributors, or take other action necessary to keep the community safe.

## Enforcement contact

The person responsible for handling enforcement is named in the repository's
issue templates and in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Prefer a private report for matters
that identify specific individuals.
